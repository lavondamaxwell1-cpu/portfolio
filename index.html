About

Trained as a full-stack developer, now building for real clients.

I earned my Full-Stack Web Development certificate through Persevere in November 2024, where I trained in building complete web applications — from the page a customer sees to the server and database behind it.

Since then I’ve kept building: a full-stack book app, an online store with secure logins and Stripe payments, a salon booking app, and business websites. I take on websites and web apps for small businesses as side work, alongside my day job.

My approach is simple: understand what the business actually needs, build it cleanly, and make sure it works well on a phone — because that’s where most customers will find it.
